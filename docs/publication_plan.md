## Publication Independence

Each manuscript must have a distinct primary research question, justified methods, and substantive independent contribution.

Related analyses may be combined when separating them would produce redundant or fragmented publications.

## Decision Criteria

Candidate papers will be assessed against:

1. Data completeness and reliability.
2. Adequacy of the sampling design.
3. Novelty and scientific significance.
4. Statistical and methodological validity.
5. Independence from other proposed papers.
6. Availability of required supplementary data.
7. Suitable journals and publication costs.

## Status

No paper is approved for full analysis until the initial data audit has been completed and the publication register has been reviewed.
