# Research Questions

## RQ1 — Ecological Composition
What are the floristic composition, diversity, abundance patterns, and community characteristics represented by the inventory?

## RQ2 — Spatial Ecology
What spatial patterns, species associations, and compositional differences can be identified from the georeferenced observations, given the sampling design?

## RQ3 — Forest Condition
Does the available evidence support comparisons of vegetation composition across disturbance levels or environmental settings?

## RQ4 — Taxonomic Data Quality
How prevalent are taxonomic inconsistencies, spelling variants, unresolved names, and other identification problems in the raw inventory?

## RQ5 — Automated Harmonization
Can a reproducible taxonomic reconciliation workflow improve name consistency while preserving uncertain identifications for expert review?

## RQ6 — Biodiversity Interoperability
How can the inventory be transformed into a well-documented, standards-compatible biodiversity dataset?

## RQ7 — Ecological Consequences of Data Cleaning
Do taxonomic and spatial quality-control procedures change estimates of richness, diversity, community composition, or other ecological conclusions?

## RQ8 — GeoAI
Can suitable environmental and remote-sensing predictors support reliable models of selected tree species, given the available sample size, spatial coverage, and validation requirements?

## Status

These questions are provisional. Each will be evaluated against the audited dataset before the corresponding analysis begins.
